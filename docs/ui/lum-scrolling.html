The valley at dawn

Before the sun rises over the ridge, all you hear in the valley is the stream and the first tits. This is the hour when the mountains belong to no one.

A forest that breathes

The spruces stand close here, one next to another. Under the crowns it is a few degrees cooler, and the moss muffles every step. The mist lingers between the trunks the longest, as if the forest did not want to let it go.

It is best to walk slowly. After a few minutes your eyes get used to the half-light and you see more: deer tracks in the mud, lichen on the bark, cones gnawed by a squirrel.

A lake like a mirror

On a windless day the water reflects the ridge so precisely that it is hard to tell where the mountain ends. One gust is enough to scatter the image into a thousand silver lines.

Higher up, only snow

Above the lake the trail turns into a gully where snow lies until June. Bring crampons and check the forecast, because up high the weather can change within fifteen minutes.

The last spruces stay below. What remains is white, the sky and your own breath.
